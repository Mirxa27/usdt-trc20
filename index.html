What it is

USDT is the ticker the market uses for a dollar-denominated unit. This deployment puts that unit on TRON as a TRC20 token so it can move in the same rails as any other TRC20 asset: send, receive, approve and pull with transferFrom. Balances are integers with 6 decimals, exactly like the canonical USDT contract, which is what wallets and routers expect.

What it is not

It is not issued by Tether and holds no claim on Tether's reserves. There is no mint on a treasury schedule and no redemption desk. Treat the peg as a target maintained by whoever runs the deployment, not as a guarantee.

Contract behaviour

  • Standard TRC20: transfer, transferFrom, approve, allowance, balanceOf, totalSupply.
  • Supply tools: mint(address,uint256), burn(uint256), burnFrom(address,uint256), plus airdrop(address[],uint256).
  • Ownership: owner and transferOwnership. The owner can mint; holders can always burn their own balance.
  • 15 functions in total. Tether's admin API (pause, blacklist, fees, issue, redeem) is not present — see the security review.

FAQ

Why does my wallet show a different icon? Wallets cache icons for hours; Trust Wallet serves them from a community repository, not from here.

Is the supply fixed? 50,000,000 was minted at deploy, but the owner can mint more. Watch the total supply, not this sentence.

Can transfers be frozen? Not at contract level — there is no pause and no blacklist in the deployed code.
